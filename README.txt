In the early days of Minecraft's development, Jeb added the squid. At the time, he based it on the cow but forgot to remove its milking behavior, letting players milk squids. In 2011, Mojang removed this unfortunate bug incredible feature. In 2026, our newest mod, Squid Milk, brings it back, along with some interesting additions.

Squid milk may look tasty at first glance - but be warned, it is a dangerous mix of ink and whatever else lies within the squid's innards. Consuming it removes all positive effects from the player. Clever players learn to weaponize squid milk by crafting a squid bomb.

Features:
- Re-texture of squid
- Squid Milk
- Squid Bomb
